## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for small business owners and independent merchants who need clarity, calm, and absolute confidence when managing expenses, invoices, and receipts. The visual narrative balances institutional financial reliability with an approachable, serene micro-business experience. It strips away the intimidating density of enterprise accounting tools in favor of tactile ease, crisp readability, and calm order.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Soft Corporate & Calming Tactility:** Combines crisp typography, gentle airy pastel surfaces, and generous rounded forms with delicate, architectural hairline borders.
- **No Stark Pure Black:** High-contrast text uses deep navy tones (`Azul 900 Noche` and `Azul 800 Institucional`) to produce an elegant, high-legibility typographic scale without harsh visual fatigue.
- **Tone:** Grounded, trustworthy, orderly, and encouraging. Receipt scans, daily expense tallies, and financial categories feel effortless and instantly readable under natural light or quick on-the-counter mobile checks.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in tranquil mist blues and restorative sage greens, engineered to keep daily bookkeeping low-stress and visually distinct.

### Color Tiers & Token Mapping
- **Background Foundations:**
  - App Canvas Default: `#F3F7FA` (Azul 50 Nube) or `#F2F8F4` (Verde 50 Niebla) for section differentiation.
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and interaction containers.
  - Surface Subdued: `#E4EDF4` (Azul 100 Hielo) and `#E3F0E8` (Verde 100 Rocío) for nested containers, badge foundations, and input backings.
- **Border & Hairline Structure:**
  - Subtle Dividers / Card Outlines: `#CDD9E6` (Azul 200 Brisa) and `#C9E1D3` (Verde 200 Menta).
- **Brand Accents & Highlights:**
  - Mist Blue: `#8FAFC8` (Azul 400 Bruma) and `#AFC5D8` (Azul 300 Cielo) for active pill tabs, soft icons, and graphical badges.
  - Sage Green: `#A6C8B4` (Verde 400 Salvia) and `#B5D5C3` (Verde 300 Pastel) for success states, revenue logs, and confirmation accents.
- **Action & Button Primaries:**
  - Primary Slate Action: `#526F8D` (Azul 600 Pizarra) with active hover/tap state `#3D5875` (Azul 700 Profundo).
  - Alternate Natural Action: `#5F927B` (Verde 600 Natural) with tap state `#39796B` (Verde 700 Petróleo).
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:**
  - High Contrast / Primary Text: `#1C2B43` (Azul 900 Noche).
  - Secondary Text / Institutional Labels: `#263A5B` (Azul 800 Institucional) and `#3D5875` (Azul 700 Profundo).
  - Tertiary / Subtle Metadata: `#6F91AD` (Azul 500 Sereno).

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a warm, highly legible, yet strictly modern geometric cadence. Its open apertures and balanced proportions ensure numbers, currency tags, and small receipts remain clear on handheld viewports.

### Typographic Rules
- **Monetary Values:** Currency figures use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so daily balances and receipt amounts align cleanly in lists and cards.
- **Display Weights:** Reserved for screen titles, totals (`headline-xl`, `currency-display`), and interactive action text to maintain instant hierarchy.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Used across tags, payment method badges, and tab bars with slight positive letter spacing to maintain crisp legibility over subtle tinted backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around an 8pt rhythmic grid system designed for effortless one-handed reachability in shop counters and on-the-go environments.

### Grid & Density
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 480px):** Single-column fluid stacked layout with `16px` (`1rem`) lateral margin and `16px` internal element gutters.
- **Card Grids:** Summary dashboards (Daily Totals, Receipt Counts) sit on 2-column equal splits with a `12px` or `16px` gap.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum interactive area is `48px` height for all primary buttons, bottom navigation anchors, input fields, and pill selectors.
- **Bottom Navigation Dock:** Fixed bottom navigation bar (`64px` height + safe area inset) keeps "Inicio", "Registros", and Quick Actions within thumb reach.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or dirty drop-shadows. Depth is articulated through surface tonal layering combined with feather-soft ambient tints.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Canvas foundation in `#F3F7FA` or `#F2F8F4`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Inputs):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid #CDD9E6`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(82, 111, 141, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(38, 58, 91, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Elements & Modals):** Camera capture framing, bottom sheets, and floating action triggers. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(38, 58, 91, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(82, 111, 141, 0.05)`.
- **Focus & States:** Interactive inputs transition their hairline border to `1.5px solid #526F8D` with a subtle outer glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(143, 175, 200, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an inviting, friendly geometry that mitigates the rigid feel of financial admin tools.

### Corner Treatment
- **Pill-Shaped / Rounded-Full:** Primary buttons, status chips, payment method toggles (Efectivo, Tarjeta, Transferencia), and tab selectors use full pill borders (`9999px`).
- **Rounded-2xl / Rounded-3xl:** Dashboard highlight cards, expense item blocks, and camera capture preview panels adopt generous curves (`16px` to `24px`).
- **Form Inputs:** Styled with smooth `14px` to `16px` radius corners for a welcoming, tactile interaction feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Slate):** Background `#526F8D`, label `#FFFFFF`, hover/active `#3D5875`. Minimum height `50px`, fully rounded pill shape.
- **Secondary Action (Natural Sage):** Background `#5F927B`, label `#FFFFFF`, hover/active `#39796B`. Used for positive actions like "Registrar Gasto" or "Confirmar Código".
- **Soft Accent / Tonal Button:** Background `#E4EDF4` with text `#263A5B`, or background `#E3F0E8` with text `#244C3E`.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Border `1.5px solid #CDD9E6`, text `#3D5875`, transparent background.

### Input Fields & Verification Boxes
- **Text & Amount Inputs:** White `#FFFFFF` or very pale `#E4EDF4` background, `1px solid #CDD9E6` border, text `#1C2B43`, placeholder text `#6F91AD`.
- **OTP Code Cells (Confirmar Código):** Individual segmented squares with `12px` rounded corners, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CDD9E6`, focused border `#526F8D`.

### Filter Chips & Segmented Controls
- **Payment Method Badges (Efectivo, Tarjeta, Transferencia, Mixto):** Pill-shaped chips. Unselected state: background `#F3F7FA`, border `1px solid #CDD9E6`, text `#526F8D`. Selected state: background `#526F8D` with white text, or `#5F927B` with white text.

### Cards & Expense List Items
- **Summary Cards (Dashboard):** `#FFFFFF` base, `1px solid #CDD9E6`, radius `20px`. Contains icon pill in `#E4EDF4` or `#E3F0E8`, metric title in `#6F91AD`, and numerical amount in `#1C2B43` using tabular numbers.
- **List Items (Últimos Gastos):** Horizontal card or divider row with circular category icon, title in `#1C2B43` (weight 600), timestamp in `#6F91AD`, and right-aligned price in `#1C2B43` (or sage green `#39796B` for balance additions).

### Receipt Camera Viewfinder (Capturar Ticket)
- Container framed with rounded corner guides (`24px`), dark tinted backdrop (`rgba(28, 43, 67, 0.4)`), guide hairline in `#8FAFC8`, and a bottom capture trigger button with glowing sage accent `#5F927B`.

### Assistant / Empty State Bubble ("Hola, soy Chek...")
- Rounded talk card (`rounded-2xl`) in `#E4EDF4` with border `1px solid #AFC5D8`, companion avatar emblem in `#5F927B`, and dialogue text in `#263A5B`.
